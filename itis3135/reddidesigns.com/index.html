Services

Reddi Designs is Neelesh Reddi's fictional web design and consulting firm. We focus on helping small businesses, community organizations, and independent creators explain what they do through clear, useful websites. Our approach brings thoughtful design together with an interest in data and practical problem-solving.

What Makes Us Different

  • Clarity comes first. We organize content around the questions visitors need answered.
  • Design with a purpose. Colors, type, and spacing reinforce a client's identity.
  • Room for every screen. We plan layouts that adapt from phones to desktop computers.
  • Accessibility from the start. We consider contrast, keyboard navigation, and meaningful image descriptions.
  • Plain-language collaboration. We explain design decisions without burying clients in technical terms.
  • Thoughtful handoffs. We keep files organized and explain how to make everyday content updates.

What We Can Build Together

We can turn an idea into a focused portfolio, a small business website, or a clear landing page. Our services cover content planning, responsive HTML and CSS, useful JavaScript interactions, and a review of navigation and accessibility. Whether a client needs a fresh start or a more readable existing site, we work toward a page that helps visitors find information and take the next step.
