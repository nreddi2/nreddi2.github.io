A board, a plan, and someone to play. The Noble Raven Starter Kit is a fictional beginner bundle for players who want a friendly introduction to chess. It brings the club's practice tools together in one portable set.

What Comes in the Kit

  • A foldable practice board and a complete 32-piece chess set.
  • Six illustrated piece cards with movement reminders.
  • A game notebook for recording moves and one lesson from each match.
  • A starter booklet with board setup and club-night etiquette.

The piece cards and club guide preview the learning material.

Built for First Games

Start without a clock, keep the movement cards beside the board, and focus on spotting one threat at a time. The notebook turns a finished game into a small, achievable practice goal.

Bring the kit to a study break or a casual game night. It is meant for conversation and repeat practice, not a promise of a particular rating.
