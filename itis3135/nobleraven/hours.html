Noble Raven Club Hours

Make Time for One Game.

A proposed weekly rhythm for the Noble Raven table.

Our fictional shop pairs kit browsing with relaxed practice sessions. The proposed schedule leaves space for first games, questions about the pieces, and short challenges that people can try without committing an entire afternoon.

First-flight sessions focus on setup and movement. Open-table evenings welcome people who know the moves and want a friendly opponent, while puzzle Saturdays give pairs something to solve together. These example hours describe the business concept; there are no real events to book.
