A Chess Table Close to Home.

A small pop-up with room for a second chair.

Picture Noble Raven as a compact chess-kit stall in a neighborhood makers market. A sample board sits at the front, the three kits fit on a shelf, and a demonstration table gives visitors a chance to try the cards before choosing a set.

Charlotte is the inspiration for this imagined location. The concept would favor an accessible indoor space near transit, with clear walking routes and seats for people who prefer to learn at a table. A real venue and street address have not been established.
