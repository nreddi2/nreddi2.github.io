Noble Raven Getting Started

Unpack. Set Up. Play.

Take your first game one step at a time.

Open the board with a light square in the near-right corner. Place the rooks in the corners, then the knights and bishops, and put each queen on her own color beside the king. Line the pawns up in front and keep the piece cards within reach.

For the first practice game, skip the clock and explain your moves to one another. Use the notebook to record one position that surprised you. When a rule raises a question, pause and check it together before continuing; the kit is meant to support the conversation.

A First-Night Routine

  1. Set up together and identify every piece.
  2. Try a few legal moves with the illustrated cards nearby.
  3. Play a friendly game with time for questions.
  4. Write down one thing to practice next time.

Check Your First Moves

Try three quick questions before your first game. Select one answer for each question, then check your answers for a score and explanations. You can try again as often as you like.

1. Which square belongs in the near-right corner?
2. Where does the queen start?
3. Which piece can jump over other pieces?
