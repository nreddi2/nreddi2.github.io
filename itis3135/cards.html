Playing Cards

Meet the Noble Raven club's six strategy cards. Each card introduces a chess piece, its movement, and one idea to try in your next game.

King

Protect the Captain

Moves one square in any direction. Keep it safe: checkmate ends the game.

When attacked, look for a safe move, a capture, or a block.

Queen

The All-Rounder

Moves any number of squares along a rank, file, or diagonal, without jumping over pieces.

Coordinate with another piece instead of attacking alone.

Rook

Own the Open File

Moves horizontally or vertically as far as the path stays clear.

An open file gives a rook room to work.

Bishop

Watch the Diagonals

Moves diagonally and stays on its starting square color for the whole game.

Look past the nearest square for long-range threats.

Knight

Find the Fork

Moves two squares in one direction and one perpendicular to it. It can jump over pieces.

Look for a move that attacks two targets at once.

Pawn

Small Steps, Big Plans

Moves forward one square and captures one square diagonally forward. Its first move can cover two clear squares.

A pawn reaching the far rank promotes to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.
