Instructions

This guide explains the Noble Raven Chess Club's game-night routine: set up a board, record moves, and review one useful lesson afterward. It is a practical starting point for new players.

Set Up | Record Moves | Take a Turn | Review a Game | Club Etiquette

Set Up the Board

Place the board so each player has a light square in the near-right corner. White starts on ranks 1 and 2, and Black starts on ranks 8 and 7.

  1. Place rooks in the corners, then knights, then bishops.
  2. Put each queen on a square matching her color, with the king beside her.
  3. Fill the next rank with pawns. White moves first.

Before starting, agree whether to use a clock and how much time each player receives.

Record Moves

Files run from a to h; ranks run from 1 to 8. A square such as e4 combines its file and rank.

  • e4: a pawn moves to e4.
  • Nf3: a knight moves to f3; N distinguishes the knight from the king.
  • Bxe5: a bishop captures on e5.
  • O-O: kingside castling, when legal.
  • + marks check and # marks checkmate.

Add a move number before White's move and record Black's reply beside it. For example: 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6.

Take a Thoughtful Turn

Check your opponent's latest threat before choosing your own move. You cannot make a move that leaves your king in check.

  1. Look for checks, captures, and threats for both sides.
  2. Choose a candidate move and imagine the opponent's strongest reply.
  3. Check that your destination is safe before moving.

Use the piece cards if you need a movement reminder. Castling, en passant, and promotion have additional rules to learn as you progress.

Review a Finished Game

Choose one turning point rather than trying to explain every move. Discuss what each player noticed and what they missed.

  • Write the move number and your original idea.
  • Find one alternative and explain its purpose.
  • Set a small practice goal for the next game.

Use the reading shelf to choose a topic that matches your goal, such as tactics or endgames.

Keep Games Welcoming

Agree on whether advice and takebacks are allowed before the game begins. Ask before offering advice during someone else's match.

Celebrate a clever idea without putting down the other player. When a rule is unclear, pause and look it up together before continuing.
