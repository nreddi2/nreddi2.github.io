A Little About Me

I was born in India and spent most of my childhood in California before moving to North Carolina in 2021. I'm now building on my interest in technology through data science and software development.

Machine learning and AI especially interest me. I'm drawn to natural language processing: working with unstructured text and turning it into something useful. I'd like to build tools that help people navigate complex information while being clear about uncertainty and the limits of the data.

Experience & Projects
